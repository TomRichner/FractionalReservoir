# Chronological IED-like model exploration

September 30, 2026. Single realizations reviewed one at a time; approximately
15 runs planned before checking in. No external input; additive Wiener noise
is present. See [the protocol and detector](README.md). Failed or unsuitable
configurations are retained. Parameters and outcomes below are experimental
observations, not manuscript claims.

Next: reduce recurrence sparsity without changing per-edge strength.
