**Why this class.** `SRNNCellTypePairs` names each synaptic route individually, so depression can be put on one route and not another. `SRNNModel2` cannot express that: its depression count is per **presynaptic population**, so it can say "all outgoing E synapses depress" but never distinguish E to E from E to I.

**Implementation note.** When a mechanism is switched off its state variables are excluded from the state vector and from the Jacobian entirely, rather than being integrated at zero. This prevents spurious zero eigenvalues from disabled dynamics.
